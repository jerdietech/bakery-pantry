Shelf-Life & Storage Guide

General freshness guidance for bakery products. For surplus items, check the bakery-verified consume-by date and storage instructions displayed at the time of sale.

🥖

Pandesal

Best consumed: within 1–2 days after baking.

Keep covered in a clean, dry container at room temperature. It may become dry or stale sooner depending on conditions.

🥐

Spanish Bread & Monay

Best consumed: within 1–2 days for Spanish bread and 2–3 days for plain monay.

Keep sealed in a clean, dry container. Follow the bakery's instructions for filled varieties.

🍞

Loaf Bread

Best consumed: within 3–5 days after opening, as a general quality guide.

Reseal the package after each use and store in a cool, dry place. Follow the package date if provided.

🧁

Ensaymada, Cheese Rolls & Muffins

Best consumed: within 1–2 days as a general guide.

Because toppings and fillings differ, check the bakery's storage label. Refrigerate when instructed, especially for perishable fillings.

🍥

Cinnamon Rolls & Brownies

Best consumed: within 1–2 days for best quality at room temperature, depending on ingredients.

Keep covered in a clean container. Follow specific storage directions for icing or dairy-based toppings.

🥧

Egg Pie & Other Custard Pastries

Storage: keep refrigerated as directed by the bakery; do not leave perishable custard fillings at room temperature for extended periods.

Check the bakery's use-by guidance before eating.

Please remember: These are general freshness estimates, not guaranteed expiry dates. Actual shelf life depends on the recipe, handling, packaging, and storage temperature. The bakery must verify and display product-specific use-by instructions and batch dates before sale. The dates on the sample surplus cards are illustrative only, not food-safety recommendations. Do not eat food with mold, unusual odor, or other signs of spoilage. A discount does not make an item safe to eat for longer.

About Pantry Bakehouse

At Pantry Bakehouse, we believe good days can start with something freshly baked. From familiar everyday breads to sweet pastries and satisfying treats, our products are made to bring a little comfort to your daily routine.

We aim to offer a welcoming selection of delicious, quality baked goods at prices that feel within reach. We also make the most of our daily products by offering selected surplus deals when available, helping good food find its way to customers.
